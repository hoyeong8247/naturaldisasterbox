Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NaturalDisasterBox (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information in connection with this website and any related content-publishing workflow.

1. Information we may process

Depending on the features used, information may include:

  • Contact information you provide when contacting us, such as your email address and message.
  • Account and authorization information required to connect a social-media account, such as access tokens and account identifiers.
  • Content and metadata submitted for transfer or publication, such as video files, captions, and publication status.
  • Basic technical information generated when you visit this website, such as browser and server log information, if collected by the hosting provider.

2. How information is used

Information may be used to respond to inquiries, operate and secure the website, authenticate an authorized social-media connection, transfer content at the account operator’s request, and troubleshoot the workflow.

3. TikTok and other service providers

If a TikTok connection is enabled, information may be processed by TikTok in accordance with TikTok’s own terms and privacy policy. The workflow may also use hosting, automation, and file-storage providers, such as n8n and Google Drive, where configured by the account operator. Their handling of information is governed by their respective policies.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers only as needed to operate the configured workflow, comply with law, protect rights and security, or at the direction of the account operator.

5. Retention and security

Information is retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described above or as required by law. Access credentials should be stored in the relevant provider’s secure credential-management features and should not be placed in public code repositories.

6. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about, correct, or request deletion of personal information we hold, subject to applicable legal requirements and technical limitations.

7. Children

This website and workflow are not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

9. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: naturaldisasterbox@app.com .
